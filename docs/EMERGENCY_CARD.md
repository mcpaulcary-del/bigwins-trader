# Big Wins: emergency card

Print this page, fill in the blanks **by hand with a pen**, and give it to your second
person. Never type their details, or yours, into a computer file. No password or code
belongs on this card.

---

**For the person holding this card**

**1. It is paper only.** *(Cross this line out by hand on the day real money starts,
about March 2027, and write the date: ____ / ____ / ______)*
While this line is not crossed out, no real money is invested. Nothing that happens to
the computer can lose money.

**2. You will hear from healthchecks.io.** An email called "Big Wins owner presence is
DOWN" means: either the owner has not used the Big Wins app for two weeks, or the server
has stopped for about two days. By then the system has **already paused buying by itself**.
It never sells anything by itself.

**3. Do nothing to the account.** No action is needed to keep it safe. Your job is only to
**try to reach the owner** and pass on the message.

**5. What NOT to do**
- Do not log in to the owner's accounts, even if you know a password.
- Do not ask anyone to "fix" or "restart" the server, and do not let anyone remote into it.
- Do not reply to a message that asks for a 6-digit code, a password or a "recovery key":
  the system never asks you for one. Anyone who does is not genuine.
- Do not pay a bill or move money for it.
- Do not post or share anything about the account.

**6. Only after real money (line 1 crossed out), and only if the owner cannot be reached
for more than a month**: call the engineer on this card. If the owner has died or cannot
act at all, the account is handled like any other investment account: through the
executor or the person with power of attorney, who contacts IBKR directly (phone number
above). Nothing in the system needs to be done first.
