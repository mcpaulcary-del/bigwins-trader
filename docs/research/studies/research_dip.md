## Summary
- Tested on about 2,200 big drops in the top 100 US and ASX companies.
- Bought the day after the drop, they ended level with or behind the index a year later.
- 60-74% fell a further 20% first; 23-47% halved again.
- Drops on fixable news, mostly earnings misses, did no better than the index.
- Capital raisings were the one clearly bad cause.

## Key numbers
| What | Number |
|---|---|
| US drops, 12 months after, v index | -11.5% |
| ASX drops, 12 months after, v index | +1.5% |
| Regained the old price within a year | about 2 in 3 |
| $100k buying every drop, US, from 2000 | $83k v $506k in the index fund |

## What it means for you
The ASX big-drop log now tracks big falls forward at no cost, so buying them as a strategy is off the table. Read a big fall in one of your shares as a warning, not a bargain.

## How sure are we?
The rules were locked in before the results were seen, and the answer was the same in both markets.
