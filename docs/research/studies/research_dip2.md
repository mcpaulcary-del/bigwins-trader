## Summary
- Designed on the US: buy a calm company's first big drop in two years, hold at least a year, keep it while it stays in an uptrend.
- Tested unchanged on the ASX, UK, Germany, France, Canada and Hong Kong.
- It was behind the index in five of six markets.
- Buying the dip in a former winner was the worst version of all.
- The one open lead is directors buying soon after a drop. It was US-only and is now being logged on the ASX.

No dip-buying line will run, on paper or with money. Keep your index fund as the core and leave this to the forward drop log.
