## Summary
- Nothing tested passed. Each idea had to lead the main line by at least 1 point a year, stay ahead in both halves of 2019-2026, and clear a luck test. None came close on luck.
- Nearest miss, conviction sizing: 12 holdings instead of 18, with $1,250 in each of the five best-scored shares and $625 in the rest. It led by 2.0 points a year after tax.
- Its winners were not more common (21.8% of holdings rose 40% or more, against 21.5%), but each winner made about $1,000 instead of $525.
- Heavy short selling is no wreck warning in ranks 301-600: almost nothing there is shorted. Heavy share issuance failed on the design years.
- Keeping +40% winners longer made more money (1.3 points a year), but fewer holdings reached +40% (17.7% against 21.5%), so it failed its own test.
- January fixes: putting idle cash above $10,000 into the A200 fund added about $1,710. Letting sales through when rule 14 blocks trading cost $660 on its own.
- Rule 14 (no decision when ranks 301-600 hold under 150 shares) blocked all trading in 42 of the last 95 month-ends, including December 2018 to June 2020.

## What it means for you
Nothing changes now. In January you will be offered the idle-cash fix, which helped, and the sales fix, a safety fix that costs a little. With about $650 a year of running costs, every version ends behind the A200 fund at $10,000. Conviction sizing could only run on paper, if a line is retired. Decide what rule 14 should be, and do not put conviction sizing on real money.

## How sure are we?
Sure that nothing passed. Every change had t 0.4 to 0.7 against the main line; the pass mark was 2.0 and the luck bar about 3.6. A true lead of 2 points a year gives an expected t of about 0.5 over eight years; t 2 would need about 8.5 points a year, so this test could not prove a small edge either way. The years 2019-2026 are in effect one path: rule 14 kept every start in cash until August 2020. Parts of conviction sizing had been seen in earlier reports, so its hold-out is not fully clean. Dividends and franking are not taxed in any book.
