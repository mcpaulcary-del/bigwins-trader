## Summary
- The rules were run unchanged in five markets, 2018-2026.
- The leaders rule lost to the local index in four of five markets, so its US lead was most likely luck (Nvidia).
- The 20-most-traded rule was slightly ahead, but nowhere near enough to count.
- The new tilted index did no better than tilting at random.
- Every overseas book and index fund trailed the S&P 500 fund by 5-10 points a year over this period.

## What it means for you
This closes the US share-picking search: the rules did not travel. Keep your index fund as the core, inside super where possible.

## How sure are we?
The rules were locked in before any overseas result was computed. The window is under nine years, so it rules out a large edge (about 3 points a year) but cannot rule out a tiny one.
