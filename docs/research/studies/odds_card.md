## Summary
- Every share page now shows how often similar shares rose 40% or more, doubled or halved within a year, from January 2005 to October 2025.
- Similar means the same size group (ranks 301-600 for Big Wins shares) and the same fifth by score.
- In ranks 301-600 the top fifth by score did best: 26.9% rose 40% or more, 11.1% doubled and 16.4% halved. In the bottom fifth: 15.7%, 6.1% and 29.0%.
- Even in the top fifth the middle result was 0%: half of these shares did no better than flat over the year.
- The card says "Odds, not a forecast. Past patterns can change." It holds counts only, no prices, and the trader never reads it.
- It tests no rule, so it adds nothing to the trial count.

## What it means for you
Use the card to set expectations for a share you hold: even among the best-scored, about one in six halved within a year. It cannot say which ones will. Do not buy or sell because of it; nothing to do.

## How sure are we?
The counts are facts about the past: about 6,000 stock-months in each band 301-600 group. But a share appears every month and nearby 12-month windows overlap, so the real sample is much smaller than the count. No costs or tax are in it, and the score was developed on part of this history. Where the two halves differ a lot, trust the odds less.
