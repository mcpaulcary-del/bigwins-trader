## Summary
- The review's verdict: build it, with changes. As an information-only archive it is cheap and harmless, and it holds no money, even on paper.
- It cannot rate news before the price reacts. Most announcements land between 8 and 10 am and the price moves that day; the archive sees them a day later.
- So the honest test is whether a share keeps drifting after the news. A first reading on that is possible in January 2028.
- Expect a wreck warning, not a winner finder: the reviewer puts a usable winner signal at about 1 in 10, and a usable wreck flag at about 1 in 4.
- The AI must beat a simple checklist (placements, trading halts, quarterly cash reports). An answer on wrecks and winners needs about three years: December 2029.
- Reading full announcements needs your permission. Today's exception covers the announcement list and two kinds of notice only, and headlines alone nearly all sound positive.
- Cost is small: roughly $20-80 a month for full text. Ratings would never be shown on your share pages.

## What it means for you
Nothing starts without you. It is waiting for your decision on reading the full text of price-sensitive announcements; headlines alone are weak. It would be information only, outside the cap on paper lines (ledger entry 96), and would never show ratings on your screens. Decide when you are ready; until then, nothing to do.

## How sure are we?
Can't tell yet: this is a design review, not a test. No back-test was run and no trial was added. The counts come from one archived day (23 Nov 2026). The timing estimates are the reviewer's arithmetic: in the first year a one-month drift shows only if it is above 4 points.
