## Summary
- Big money cannot come from $10,000. Even a very good 15% a year turns $10,000 into about $20,000 in five years.
- Three months of paper trading tests the machinery, not the edge. Nor will 24 months prove an edge this small.
- The biggest danger is behavioural: with about 24 paper lines, one will be far ahead by February by pure luck, and it will be tempting to back it.
- There were too many paper lines. The cut list should be fixed before any results exist. (Done on 24 November.)
- Two faults must be fixed before real money: the rule that can block sales in a data gap, and idle cash above $10,000.
- "Beats its random twins" is a low bar, because random books lose 4-8% a year to costs. Beating A200 after all costs is the test that matters.
- The go-live decision should rest on whether the machinery works, never on three months of results.

## What it means for you
Wealth will come from how much you save and invest in the index fund, not from this system. Putting in $50,000 is the only way the numbers work, and nothing justifies it before late 2028 at the earliest. Think of the $10,000 as tuition: $10,000 at risk plus about $1,000 a year, for a number of years you decide now.

## How sure are we?
This is a review of the project's own figures; no new tests were run. The cost and size arithmetic is solid. What happens next is not knowable in advance, which is the point of the review.
