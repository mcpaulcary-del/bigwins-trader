## Summary
- Saving 25% of take-home gives about a 50% chance of retiring at 62; 40% gives about 55.
- Big Wins today sits in cash about half the time; the January fixes take it from behind the index to about 1.4 points a year ahead after tax, before running costs.
- Running costs make a $10k book a loss; break-even is about $36k.
- Employer super already fills the yearly concessional cap at our salaries.
- Sensible size for Big Wins: at most 5-8% of investments, ideally in super.

## Key numbers
| What | Number |
|---|---|
| Retire at 50% chance: save 10% / 25% / 40% | 70 / 62 / 55 |
| Big Wins today v index, 25 years | -1.7 points a year |
| With January fixes, after 47% tax | +1.4 points a year |
| Break-even account | about $36k |

## What it means for you
Pick your savings rate and automate it into an index fund. Take the accountant brief to a meeting. Decide on the January fixes on 1 January.

## How sure are we?
The savings maths is solid. The Big Wins edge is not proven: showing a 1.5-point edge for certain would take far longer than any paper test.
