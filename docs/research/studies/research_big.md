## Summary
- Doubling is common for single shares: 8.8% of ASX shares doubled over any 12 months since 2005. But 20.9% halved, and the typical share lost 8.5%.
- Two-thirds of tradeable doublers start in ranks 301-1000. The pond is right; the wrecks that come with it are the problem.
- Everything that finds more winners market-wide finds as many wrecks: cheap shares, new listings, volatile shares, hot themes.
- The one exception is the existing score inside ranks 301-600: 10.4-11.8% doublers against 6.9-7.4%, with fewer wrecks.
- A computer model built to forecast winners learnt to buy lottery tickets. Its book lost 90%.
- Buying shares that have already doubled and holding them loses: three years later the typical one is down 43%.
- Holding 5 shares instead of 18 lifted the share of +50% years from 6% to 30%, and the worst fall from 67% to about 75%.

## What it means for you
Big wins happen to single shares, not to whole accounts. To own enough of a winner to double the account you must hold so few shares that losing 70% is just as likely. Three aggressive lines now run on paper to watch this in real time. None gets real money before December 2028. Do not chase cheap, new or themed shares for the upside.

## How sure are we?
Sure that no rule gave reliable 50-200% years: it did not happen once in 20 years of history for 13 rules. Not sure the aggressive rules beat the current one: their lead (t about 2) is below the luck bar (about 3.6), and the back-tested growth is the best of 13 tries, so it is overstated.
