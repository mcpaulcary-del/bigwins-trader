## Summary
- Designed on 2000-2014, then tested once on 2015-2026, years it had not seen.
- The leaders rule grew at 14.0% a year after tax held personally, against 12.0% for the S&P 500 fund and 16.2% for a Nasdaq-100 fund.
- It lost to the S&P 500 fund by 3.5 points a year in 2021-2026.
- Nvidia was its biggest holding in 11 of 12 start dates. Without Nvidia it trailed the fund.
- Its falls were about twice the fund's: 30% against 16%.
- Anthropic is a private company. Its shares cannot be bought on any market.

## What it means for you
No US share-picking line will be run. An index fund already owns every future giant at full weight and lets it run without tax on the way up. A tech tilt means a small fixed slice of an Australian-listed Nasdaq-100 fund, sized for a fall of 30-80%. Keep your index fund as the core.

## How sure are we?
Every lead flipped between the design years and the test years, so none can be trusted. The best result had a t of 2.3 against a luck bar of 3.6. The rules are frozen and can be read again from public prices in December 2028 at no cost.
