# Pre-registration — report 51, company-level predict-vs-actual study

Written 2026-11-14 BEFORE any stock return, signal or forward outcome was computed.
At the time of writing I had only listed the data directories and read five rows of one daily file.
This file is hashed (SHA-256 in `out/prereg_hash.txt`) before the first run. Any later change is

## 1. Data

- D1. `rs77/asx-historical-data` (public GitHub, commit 5fd4f5b0…, no licence file), daily
  `code,date,open,high,low,close,volume`, unadjusted, 1997-01 to 2024-05-31, all codes incl. delisted.
  Plus its `company_details/delisted` (2000–2020, with reasons) and `changed_name` lists.
- D2. stockanalysis.com JSON history (5-year daily with split-adjusted close `c` and
  dividend-adjusted close `a`; monthly max; chart max close) for the codes that matter:
  every code in the top 350 by traded value at any month-end from 2021-06 to 2024-05, the current
  ASX list by market cap (top ~450), and all case-study names. Used (a) to extend prices from
  2024-06 to 2026-11, (b) to validate/derive split factors, (c) to estimate dividends.
- D3. Index: XJO (price), XJOA/XNT (total return) from D1; after 2024-05 the A200 ETF (price and
  dividend-adjusted) from D2.
- Securities kept: codes of exactly 3 characters, not an index (zero volume) and not on the
  exchange-traded-product exclusion list (`code/etp_list.txt`, built from ASX's monthly
  investment-products report and D1 names before any return is computed). LICs and REITs stay in.
- Entity rule: a gap of more than 200 calendar days with no rows starts a new entity (code reuse);
  signals never use data from before the gap. Code changes listed in `changed_name` are linked
  (old code's history carried to the new code) when the old code's last row and the new code's
  first row are within 10 calendar days.
- Splits/consolidations: (i) where D2 has the code, the factor is the step in (raw close ÷ D2
  split-adjusted close); (ii) otherwise a heuristic: close-to-close ratio within 4% of a clean ratio
  (2,3,4,5,6,7,8,10,12,15,20,25,30,40,50,100, 1.5, 2.5 and inverses) with |ln ratio| ≥ ln 1.45, and
  day-t share volume not above 6 × ratio-scaled median of the prior 20 days (a crash on huge
  volume is not a split), and no reversion of more than half the step within 10 days.
  Heuristic (ii) is scored against (i) on the overlap and the precision/recall reported.
  Demergers, capital returns, rights-issue bonus elements are NOT adjusted (flagged).
- Dividends: D1 is price-only. Main results are price-only against the price index (like for
  like). A total-return estimate is added where D2 `a`/`c` exists (survivors only, 2016+ monthly,
  2021-11+ daily) and reported separately with its survivorship caveat.
- Delisting inside a holding period: main = exit at the last traded (adjusted) close, proceeds in
  cash at 0% to the period end. Sensitivity "fail = −100%": if the ASX delisting reason contains
  liquidat / administrat / receiver / wound / listing fees / deed of company / removed … rule 17.,
  or (no reason on file and last price < 50% of entry price), the return is −100%.

## 2. Universe and dates

- Formation dates: last trading day of December 2000 … 2025 (main, 26 dates) and of June 2001 …
  2026 (mid-year variant, reported separately, never pooled as independent evidence).
- Universe at each formation date: entities with ≥ 200 traded days in the trailing 252 trading
  days, ranked by median daily traded value (close × volume) over those 252 days.
  Main = top 300. Variants = top 200, and ranks 1–500 (for case studies of small caps only).
- Trade timing: signal at formation-day close; entry at the close of the NEXT trading day; exit at
  the close of the trading day after the next formation date (≈12 months). Index measured the same way.
- The last cohort (Dec 2025, and Jun 2026) is incomplete and is shown but excluded from averages.

## 3. Models (all fixed; higher score = predicted winner)

| ID | Name | Score at formation date t (adjusted closes; month-ends) |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | 12-1 momentum | P(t−1m) ÷ P(t−12m) − 1 |
| M2 | 6-month momentum | P(t) ÷ P(t−6m) − 1 |
| M3 | 52-week-high proximity | P(t) ÷ max close over trailing 252 trading days |
| M4 | Trend filter | P(t) ÷ mean of the last 10 month-end closes (binary version: > 1) |
| M5 | Low volatility | −(st. dev. of daily log returns, trailing 252 trading days, ≥ 200 obs) |
| M6 | 1-month reversal | −(P(t) ÷ P(t−1m) − 1) |
| M7 | 36-month reversal | −(P(t) ÷ P(t−36m) − 1) |
| M8 | Size (small) | −ln(median daily traded value, trailing 252 days) — liquidity proxy, no share counts in the data |
| M9 | Combination A | mean of cross-sectional percentile ranks of M1, M2, M3, M5 (all four required) |
| M10 | Combination B | mean of percentile ranks of all of M1–M8 that exist (≥ 6 required) |
| R0 | Random control | 1,000 random top-10 lists per date (seed 51) |

No parameter is tuned. No other model will be added after results are seen; if one is, it is
logged as post-hoc in section 9 and in `out/trials.csv`.

## 4. Predictions and outcome measures (per model × formation date)

- Predicted top decile / bottom decile of the universe by score; "predicted top 10" (and top 4, top 20).
- Actual: 12-month forward price return of every universe stock.
- Hit rate: share of predicted top-10 in the actual top decile and top quintile of the universe
  (chance = 10% / 20%); also share in the actual bottom decile, and share that lost > 50%.
- Rank IC: Spearman correlation of score with forward return, per date; mean, t-stat across dates,
  share of dates positive.
- Spreads (equal weight): top decile − bottom decile; top decile − XJO price index; top decile −
  equal-weight universe; top-10 − XJO. Mean, t-stat (26 annual observations), share of years positive.
- Binary trend (M4): stocks above vs below their 10-month average.
- Persistence: share of this year's top 10 / top decile that is in next year's; Spearman of scores
  year to year; share of last year's ACTUAL top-decile stocks that repeat in the actual top decile.
- Family-B bar from the spec: t ≥ 3.0. With 10 models the Bonferroni bar at 5% two-sided is t ≈ 3.1 (25 d.f.).

## 5. Costs, account sizes, tax

- Portfolios: A$5,000 → top 4; A$25,000 → top 10; A$100,000 → top 20. Equal weight, annual rebalance
  to the new list (names that stay are trimmed/topped up only if weight is off by > 25% relative).
- Commission per trade: max(A$6.60, 0.088% of value) (IBKR, as given in the brief).
- Half-spread per side: max(base, 0.5 × tick ÷ price), base = 0.10% if the stock is in the top 100
  by traded value, else 0.25%; tick = A$0.001 below A$0.10, A$0.005 below A$2, A$0.01 otherwise.
- Whole shares are not modelled (flagged). Idle cash earns 0%.
- Tax (approximate): net realised gains each year taxed at 32% (30% + Medicare). Two regimes, as in
  report 38: (A) 50% discount if held > 12 months; (B) no discount, 30% floor. A 12-month annual
  rebalance is assumed to qualify as "held 12 months" only in the variant where the sale is delayed
  to 12 months + 1 day (the t+1 exit does this); losses carried forward. Index buy-and-hold pays
  CGT only at a final sale. Dividend tax and franking are ignored on both sides (price-only).
- Owner-plan comparison: A$5,000 + A$500 a month from Jan 2001 (and from each later start year),
  (i) into the index (XJO price, and total return), contributions invested quarterly, one trade
  each; (ii) into model M1 / M9 top-N with N = 4 below A$15,000, 10 below A$60,000, 20 above;
  contributions held in cash and invested quarterly into the most underweight holding; annual
  re-formation. Terminal wealth before and after tax; spread across start years.

## 6. Walk-forward selection (report-48 style)

## 8. What would count as "survives"

A model survives only if, on the 25 completed December cohorts: mean top-decile-minus-XJO > 0 with

## 9. Deviations (appended after the hash; each with date and reason)

### 9.1 — 2026-11-14, before any return or outcome table was read (data-cleaning only)

- D-a. Split heuristic as first written flagged ~13,000 "splits", almost all sub-5-cent stocks moving one
  tick (0.002 → 0.003 is a "1.5 ratio"). Refined: ratios below 5 are accepted only when both closes are
  ≥ A$0.05; consolidations of 5× or more are accepted with a 15% tolerance (they trade under a
  deferred-settlement code for a week, so the price drifts across the gap); splits of 5× or more get a
  10% tolerance when the rows are ≥ 3 trading days apart. Scored against stockanalysis (table V1).
- D-b. stockanalysis chart history has gaps; where a split step falls inside a gap the step date is taken
  from the raw series (the day whose close-to-close move best matches the step).
- D-c. "removed … rule 17." in the failure keyword list is narrowed to 17.12 / 17.15 (17.11 is removal
  at the entity's own request, usually after a takeover).
- D-d. Code changes after April 2020 and before 2001 (no ASX change list in D1) are linked by a
  price-and-volume continuity rule (new code starts the trading day after the old code ends, first
  close within 10%, volume within 4×). 255 links; listed in `out/stage2_log.txt`.
- D-e. stockanalysis has no history for ASX codes delisted before today. Stocks delisted between
  June 2024 and November 2026 therefore exit at their last 31 May 2024 price and are counted as
  "censored" (reported per cohort).
