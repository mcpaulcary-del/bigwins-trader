Hashed (SHA-256) and appended to `LEDGER.jsonl` **before the code is written and before any CXL decision is computed**. First decision **Mon 30 Nov 2026**, fills at the close of Tue 1 Dec 2026. PAPER ONLY. No real money, no broker orders. The AI never places trades. EODHD data are used under a private, non-commercial licence; ASIC short-position files are public and archived privately.
None of the existing rules (v1.3 + amendments; SHADOW M3 v1.1, E3, ACC) is changed by this amendment.

## 1. Status and purpose
**SHADOW: not adopted; tracked for information. Not funded.** Report 81 §4.4: D2 failed its look (replication mean +0.2 / +0.3 points a year after tax at A$25k / A$100k; beat a random 30% exclusion in only 4 of 17 sets). It is tracked because it is the only construction that removes the −1.2-point size handicap diagnosed in report 81 §1, with the lowest tracking error (about 5 points on ASX) and turnover (about 0.3). Its forward record tests that diagnosis directly. **Expect it to sit within a point of the index fund.** It is not one of the designs that can meet `AMENDMENT_adopt_rule_v2.md`; a pass of any bar would only allow a new written proposal with its own card and clock.

## 2. Rules (report 81 §4.5, CXL v1.0, verbatim in substance; operational readings in *italics*)
1. **Clock.** Decide at each ASX month-end after the close, from Mon 30 Nov 2026. Trade at the next trading day's close: sells first, then buys, then top-ups.
2. **Universe.** The 100 most-traded companies (rules v1.3 B1, v1.4 traded value, duplicates removed by the returns test A7(c) of `AMENDMENT_v1.6_dup_returns.md`, `_old` symbols admitted per Amendment v1.5) that have a market cap. *This is the SHADOW M3 table of the same month-end (`shadow/forward_M3_snapshot_<date>.csv`, written by the M3 line from the same bundle); CXL reads that file. No table = no CXL decision that month (data block, as ACC K7).*
3. **Market cap.** Latest ASIC "total product in issue" on or before the decision day (not older than 45 days) × the day's close. A cap is ignored if yearly traded value ÷ cap is outside 0.02–20. *Source: the daily ASIC short-position files (`RR<yyyymmdd>-<vvv>-SSDailyAggShortPos.csv`) already archived every weekday by the scheduled ASIC archive job into the scratchpad folders `asic_archive/`, `asic_daily/` and `asic_dl/` (read only; copy in OneDrive "Forward archive - ASIC short positions"). The file used is the one with the latest position date on or before the decision day among the files archived when the decision is computed (highest version of that date); its name and SHA-256 are written into the list file and the ledger. Its age must be ≤ 45 calendar days, or there is no CXL decision that month (data block). Shares are matched on the ASIC product code = the ASX code; `XXX_old` codes have no ASIC match. "The day's close" = the decision day's raw close (a company with no close that day has no cap that month). Yearly traded value = the M3 table's 252-day median daily traded value × 252. A company absent from the ASIC file (no reported short position) has no cap. Coverage check at 16 Nov 2026 (13 Nov file): 100 of 100 universe companies have a cap and pass the turnover test.*
4. **Loser screen.** M3 score = mean of the percentiles (inside the 100) of: close(ME−1) ÷ close(ME−12) − 1; close ÷ close(ME−6) − 1; close ÷ highest close of 252 days *(as coded in `paper_shadow.rankings`)*. A company in the bottom 30% of the scored companies, or without a score, is out. *Kept: M3 rank ≤ 0.7 × number scored (rank 1 = best; as `deep81/code/designs.py` d2).*
5. **List.** Order the rest by market cap (largest first; *ties: lower traded-value rank*). n = min(30, max(4, floor(account ÷ A$2,500))). *Account = the book's NAV at the decision close; n is set at every decision.*
6. **Buy.** When fewer than n names are held *(after this decision's sells; a sale that waits under rule 8 still counts as held)*: buy the largest listed companies not held, down to list rank n, each at its share of the combined market cap of holdings plus purchases (no name above 15%; excess spread pro rata), scaled to the account. *Target of a name = weight × NAV × m ÷ n, with m = holdings + purchases (as `sim81.py`); a holding's cap is computed the same way (ASIC shares × close); a name with no cap gets the median cap of the names.* Order = min(target, free cash); skipped if under A$1,000 or under half the target. Price ≥ A$0.50; median traded value ≥ 100 × order. *Free cash at the decision includes the estimated proceeds of the day's sell orders; at the fill each order is again min(order, free cash) and is dropped if under its minimum.*
7. **Top-up.** When a purchase is made and n names are held *(holdings after sells + purchases ≥ n)*, a holding below 75% of its cap-weight target, by at least A$1,000, is topped up from free cash, smallest first. *The order rules of 6 apply (≥ A$1,000; price ≥ A$0.50; median traded value ≥ 100 × order, so a holding outside the 100 is not topped up).*
8. **Sell.** A holding is sold in full when it has been outside the largest 1.5 × n of the list (rule 5, after the loser screen) at two month-ends running. If it is in profit and its oldest parcel is under 12 months old *(fewer than 365 days before the decision day)*, the sale waits *(decided again each month-end)*. **No trims; no other sell rule.**
9. **Payouts, tax reserve, dividends:** as rules v1.3 C1, F, I. Free cash = cash − tax owed. *As the SHADOW M3 line codes them: payout at the last close after 20 trading days without a close; A7 code changes carry a holding (the code-change map is the H82 decision file of the day, as ACC); company dividends are cash on the ex-date; the tax owed for the financial year (32%, 50% discount for parcels held ≥ 367 days, dividends taxed) is reserved, not paid out of the book, and is deducted in the after-tax values of the scorecard (`AMENDMENT_scoring_v2.md` §4).*
10. **Record:** A$25,000 and A$100,000 books; comparator A200 at A$0 brokerage; reported sold and kept; 100 random-exclusion books beside it.

**Where §4.5 and the look code (`deep81/code/sim81.py`) differ, §4.5 is followed:** order-size liquidity test (§4.5: × order; code: × equal-weight target), top-ups only when a purchase is made (code: also on months without one), no "in the list this month" condition on top-ups, "12 months" = 365 days (code: 360). The look also reinvested dividends free and ran monthly; the forward books run daily on the same arithmetic as the M3 shadow books.

**Random-exclusion controls** (report 81 §4.4 "percentile of random books"): identical rules, except that rule 4 excludes a *random* 30% of the scored companies instead of the momentum losers. Book i of size s draws, at each decision, one `numpy.random.default_rng` permutation of the scored rows (in M3 table order) from its own persistent stream (seed 8125000 + i for A$25k, 8200000 + i for A$100k, i = 1…100); row at permutation position k gets random rank k + 1; kept: random rank ≤ 0.7 × number scored. Cap order, cap weights and everything else unchanged.

## 4. Scoring
Rows marked SHADOW CXL on `SCORECARD.md` / `scorecard_latest.csv` / `scorecard_history.csv`: NAV, time-weighted return, vs A200 (A$0), after tax on the **kept** and **sold** bases against the A200 comparator's kept and sold (`AMENDMENT_scoring_v2.md` §4), holdings, cash, n, the share of the 100 random books of the same size with a lower return ("% of random"), tracking error against A200 once 12 monthly returns exist; median and 5th–95th percentile of the random books. State file `out/paper_state/cxl_state.json`; month-end list `shadow/cxl_list_<date>.csv` (universe, ASIC shares and file, cap, turnover test, screen, list rank) and orders `shadow/cxl_orders_<date>.csv` for the two CXL books (hashes in the daily ledger entry). Hooked into `paper_daily.py` after the M3/E3 and ACC steps; never reads or writes the H82 state or the other shadow states.

## 5. What would count as information (no adopt bar)
Read at 36 months (31 Dec 2029) and each December after, information only: after tax (sold) against A200 (kept), tracking error, turnover, and the percentile among the random-exclusion books. The question it answers: does a cap-weighted book of large companies with momentum losers removed stay level with the fund at lower tracking error than H82, and does the momentum screen beat a random screen?
