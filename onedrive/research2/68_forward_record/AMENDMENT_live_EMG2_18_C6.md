## 2. Rule L4-C6 (sell), replacing rule L4 in state EMG2; everything not listed here is unchanged
- **L4-C6.1 (holding age).** A holding's age at a decision is the number of **month-ends** after the decision session at which it was bought, up to and including the current decision session (the purchase decision counts as zero; a month-end is the last ASX session of a calendar month). Every month-end counts, whether or not the book made a decision there (a rule-14 data block, or L6's skipped start month-end, still counts), so that the paper book and its simulated twin always see the same age. A holding sold in full and bought again later starts again from zero. This is `months_held` of the frozen engine (`acct3` `nme - bm`). A holding that came from a corporate action without a buy of its own (a demerged or in-specie share) counts as old enough (it was never chosen by the rule; rule L4 would sell it at once if not on T).
- **L4-C6.2 (keep zone K).** At a decision, K is the members of band C (EMG2 v1.0 rules 2-3 as in force: class rule v2, LICs/funds left out of the ranks) whose score percentile in band C is **above 0.60** (the top 40% by the EMG2 score; T, the top fifth, is inside K). Same band-C percentile as the list itself.
- **L4-C6.3 (sell).** In state EMG2, a holding is sold in full at a decision only if **both**: (a) its age is **six or more**; and (b) it is **not in K** (score percentile 0.60 or lower, or it is no longer in band C, or it has left the eligible set E). A holding that fails either test is kept and re-tested at each decision.
- **Exceptions, unchanged (sold whatever the age):** a holding flagged `BID_PEG` is sold at once (L4); a holding without a close for 20 sessions is valued and booked as a payout (BW-UNI-21), not sold.
- **Protection switch unchanged (L15).** At a decision in state FUND every share is sold (BT3) whatever its age, the cash goes into A200, and BF-D34's leftover sale and fund sweep apply. At a decision in state EMG2 a fund holding is always sold (BT4) whatever its age. After the switch turns back to EMG2 the book buys from T as registered and the ages start again.
- **Note on the owner's wording.** The owner's note lists "leaving the eligible set/band downward" with the forced sells. The frozen rule (and `AMENDMENT_shadow_EMG2_COMBO.md` L4-C (b)) treats leaving band C or the eligible set as part of test (b): such a holding is sold once it is six month-ends old, not at once. This amendment follows the frozen rule, which is what reports 99 and 100 tested; the owner may overrule by a later entry.
- **Buying unchanged** (L2, L3, L5): names of T, best first, lottery and pegged names skipped, while fewer than n = min(18, floor(min(account, A$10,000) / A$550)) names are held. With C6 the book can hold more names than n after a fall in value; it then buys nothing, as before.
