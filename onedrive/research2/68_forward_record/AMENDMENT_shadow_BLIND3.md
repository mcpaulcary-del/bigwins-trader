## 2. Rules (PREREG_BLIND3 section 1, unchanged)
At the close of the last session of every calendar month (orders fill at the next session's close):
1. **Tradable set.** Ordinary shares with a close today and at least 200 priced sessions in the last 252. Place = rank of 252-session median traded value among ordinary shares that traded in the last 5 sessions. Universe = place ≤ 400. Keep set = place ≤ 800.
2. **Calm half.** Volatility = standard deviation of daily total returns over the last 252 sessions. Buy set = universe members at or below the universe median volatility.
3. **Score** = total return from 6 months (126 sessions) to 1 month (21 sessions) ago.
4. **Sell** a holding if (a) it is no longer in the keep set, or (b) 30 or more buy-set names score higher — except that under (b) a holding in profit (value > cost) held fewer than 367 days is kept.
5. **Buy** the highest-scoring buy-set names not held and not sold today until 15 names are held or cash runs out; each parcel max(1/15, A$520 ÷ account value) of the account (A$520 floor binds below A$7,800). No top-ups, no trims, no market timing.

- Forward differences from the kit, forced by not knowing the future: a sell with no close on the fill day stays open for up to five sessions and fills at the first close (the kit fills at the last close if the share trades within five sessions); a holding without a traded close for more than 20 sessions is paid out at the next decision by the kit's rule for an unrecorded delisting (last close if within 30% of its 250-session high, else zero) even if it might resume trading later; a twin's random score is kept in the state for the ordinary shares placed ≤ 800 (the only ones the design can hold or buy) instead of being drawn for the whole panel in advance. A month-end with the H82 market-wide data block, or with fewer than 300 valid ordinary shares in the top 400, is no decision (holdings kept).
- Known data risk: an in-specie distribution (e.g. BHP's Woodside shares, June 2022) arrives in the daily bulk file as a price fall with no dividend or split row, and the vendor later folds it into a back-adjusted price history without an event. The paper books would book it as a loss; noted on the scorecard if seen.
