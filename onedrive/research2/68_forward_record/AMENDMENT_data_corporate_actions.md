# Amendment (data procedure): a hand-kept corporate-action table for in-specie distributions and capital returns, and the `CA_SUSPECT` check

Status: **REGISTERED 19 Nov 2026 — data procedure for the paper record**, by the record keeper, on report 92 (section 5.2 and section 7 item 5), before the first forward event it governs. It is a **data correction under a registered procedure, not a rule change**: no line's rules, books, thresholds or decisions change; a confirmed event is booked as what it was (a distribution), instead of as a loss. It applies to every paper line at once (H82 and its twins, the SHADOW lines, EMG2, EARLY, the EMG2-18 paper twins, BLIND3, PAIR, the comparators). It does not touch the trading system (`/home/user/asx-trader`) or the live EMG2-18 rules; section 5 lists the trading-system items for its owner. The AI never places trades.

## 1. Why (report 92 section 5.1)
An in-specie distribution hands shareholders shares in another company; the parent's price falls by their value. With no record of the event, a price file books a loss that did not happen: BHP's Woodside shares (25 May 2022) −10% on the research panel; Link's PEXA shares (30 Dec 2022) −40%; Premier Investments' Myer shares (21 Jan 2025) −27% in **both** data files. The daily bulk file the paper record books from shows such a day as a raw price fall with no dividend or split row (record-keeper check of 19 Nov, ledger seq 54 section R). Forward, an uncorrected event looks like a crash in one holding; it can cause a wrong sale (BLIND3 and PAIR member B screen on volatility and rank on momentum) and bar a re-entry.

## 2. The table: `68_forward_record/CORPORATE_ACTIONS.csv`
One row per event. Columns: `code`, `ex_date`, `book_on` (the trading day the paper books are credited; = ex_date when entered in time), `kind` (in specie / demerger / capital return), `value_per_share` (A$ of what was distributed per share of the parent: the first close of the distributed share × the distribution ratio, or the company's stated value; any cash part that is NOT already in the vendor's dividend file for that day), `distributed` (code received), `source` (the ASX announcement), `entered` (date), `status` (`pending` or `confirmed`), `note`.
- Kept by the record keeper. **Every change is hashed and noted in the ledger** (kind `data`), with the reason.
- Only `confirmed` rows with a `book_on` date have any effect. `pending` rows are records only.
- At registration the table holds the five named events of report 92 (BHP May 2022, Link Dec 2022, Premier Investments Jan 2025, Tabcorp May 2022, St Barbara Jul 2023) as `pending`, values not yet read from the announcements. They pre-date the paper record and matter only for back-tests (section 4 item 3).

## 3. Procedure
1. **Detection, every session** (`bigwins_forward/code/ca_check.py <date>`, read-only): every code held, or with an open order, in a main paper book is flagged `CA_SUSPECT` when its raw close fell 8% or more since its previous close (within 5 sessions) and the day has no dividend row, no split row and no table row. `--twins` also watches the random twins. The record keeper checks each flag against the company's ASX announcements ("in specie", "demerger", "scheme", "return of capital"); nearly all are real falls and are cleared. Once the licensed announcements feed exists (owner decision D-6), a flag is also raised by announcement title.
2. **Treatment when confirmed.** A table row with the value per share and `book_on` = the ex-date, or, if the ex-date has already been booked, the next session not yet booked (a late correction; the note says so). On `book_on`, every paper line credits **shares held before that day × value per share as cash**, through its existing dividend rule (`paper_lib.load_day` adds the table value to that day's dividend entry for the code). The paper books do not hold the new shares. No A$6.60 is charged for selling them (the per-share credit cannot carry a per-holding fee; at A$6.60 the difference is immaterial to the reading). For tax, each line treats the credit as it treats a dividend; the real treatment (cost-base reduction or income) is for the owner's accountant.
3. **Resolve before the next decision.** A `CA_SUSPECT` flag on a holding must be confirmed or cleared before the month-end decision. If it is not, the decision runs on the data as they are and the record keeper notes it on the scorecard. A flag never silently blocks a decision.
4. **The scorecard** lists each booked event (book, code, value). Until that is coded (section 4, TODO 2), the credit is visible in each book's dividend record and the record keeper notes it in the ledger entry that confirms the row.
