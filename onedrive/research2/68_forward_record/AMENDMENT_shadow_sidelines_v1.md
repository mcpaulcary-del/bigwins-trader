### SWEEP — books `EMG2_18_SIM_BT_C6_SWEEP`, twins `EMG2_18_BT_C6_SWEEP_RND_s001..s100`
- **Rule:** `EMG2_18_SIM_BT_C6` unchanged, except that **cash above A$10,000 buys A200 at each decision (A$500 minimum)**. Exactly: the account is the trading book (run by the frozen rules, which see only it: NAV, sizing min(NAV, A$10,000), cap, fund room, sweeps) plus a **sweep sleeve** of A200 units that the frozen rules never see and never sell. At each month-end decision that is not a data block (state ON or OFF), after the frozen rules have set that decision's orders, X = value of the trading book at the decision close − A$10,000. If X + the sleeve's cash ≥ A$500, then at the fill close (after the trading book's own fills, sells then buys) min(X, the trading book's cash − A$50) is moved from the trading book to the sleeve (never below zero), and the sleeve's cash buys whole A200 units at that close (IBKR brokerage, 0.05% half-spread, units cut to the cash), only if the units are worth ≥ A$500; otherwise the cash waits in the sleeve for the next decision. No A200 close on the fill day: tried at each later session's close until filled or the next decision. A200 distributions on sleeve units are sleeve cash. The sleeve is never sold.
- **Reading, monthly, no bar — arithmetic, not evidence:** account value, sleeve value, `SWEEP − BT_C6` in A$ and %, sweep orders and their costs, twin-pair percentile (reported, not tested).

## 1. EST-C (information only; no book, no trades)
- **Data:** the band-C snapshot (section 3), read exactly as `AMENDMENT_info_EST_revisions.md` s2 reads the top-300 one: file `estimates_pit/est_C_YYYY-MM-DD.json.gz`, its SHA-256 checked against `est_C_manifest.csv` (mismatch = missing); "snapshot for a date D" = the file dated D, else the latest earlier file within 5 ASX sessions; current-year estimate = the `trend` entry with `period == "0y"` and the latest `date` (FY label), value `earningsEstimateAvg`, count `earningsEstimateNumberOfAnalysts`.
- **Universe at month-end M:** every code in the band-C snapshot for M (ranks 301–600), codes whose `trend` is not a dict excluded.
- **Signal (the registered EST reading):** S = the session 63 sessions before M; signal = (estimate at M − estimate at S) ÷ raw close at S, left out when either snapshot is missing, an estimate is missing, **the analyst count is below 2 at S or at M**, the FY label differs, or the start close is missing or ≤ 0. One difference from EST, stated so: the start close is the raw close of the **last ASX session on or before the start snapshot's date** (the snapshots are taken before the open, and a snapshot can be dated a Saturday).
- **Next-month return:** raw close on M to raw close on the next month-end, dividends and confirmed `CORPORATE_ACTIONS.csv` values as cash (not reinvested), splits applied to the share count; no close at the next month-end = last close plus cash; no close on M = left out. Prices from the paper record's bulk files (`paper_lib.load_day`).
- **Measure, one a month:** codes with a signal and a return sorted by signal (ties by code, ascending); k = floor(n ÷ 3); **top third minus bottom third** = mean return of the k highest signals − mean return of the k lowest. Fewer than 30 codes with signal and return = month missing, not filled. First possible signal month 26 Feb 2027 (S = 25 Nov 2026; the first band-C snapshot is dated 24 Nov 2026).
- **Bar (wording fixed now):** when 24 monthly measures exist (expected signal months Feb 2027 – Jan 2029, read in March 2029): "signal present (information only)" iff t = mean ÷ (sd ÷ √24) ≥ 2.0 **and** the mean of the first 12 measures > 0 **and** the mean of the second 12 > 0; else "not detected". No other cut is evidence.

## 2. DIL-C (information only)
- **At each month-end M from 30 Nov 2026** (decision or data block; any month-end with a bundle): the band-C names of the month-end table (`paper_emg2.table` C: eligible, ranks 301–600). **Shares on issue** from the band-C snapshot for M (section 3): the vendor's `outstandingShares::quarterly` history; N1 = the entry with the latest `dateFormatted` ≤ M; N0 = the entry whose `dateFormatted` is nearest to 365 days before N1's date, within ±62 days (tie: the earlier). g = N1 ÷ N0 − 1. **Flagged iff g > 0.20**; unflagged iff g ≤ 0.20; no N1 or N0 (or ≤ 0) = left out.
- **Returns:** as EST-C, from the close on M to the close on the 6th and the 12th month-end after M. Cohort difference = mean return of flagged − mean of unflagged; missing when fewer than 5 flagged or 20 unflagged names have the return.
- **Bar:** reading at 24 months (last ASX session of November 2028; cohorts whose horizon has closed by then), second reading at 36 months: "dilution signal present (information only)" iff **both** the 6-month and the 12-month mean differences are negative with Newey–West t ≤ −2 (Bartlett, lags 5 and 11 for the overlapping horizons); else "not detected".
