## What blocks the build

1. **The breaker rejects legitimate full exits and latches a halt (F-01).** A sleeve that has drifted more than 5% above its base weight cannot be sold out; the attempt halts the account.
2. **Halts block risk-off sells, and three of the halt triggers fire in exactly the conditions where the rule wants to sell (F-02, F-03, F-04).** Recommendation is in the next section.
3. **A missed or blocked evaluation has no defined behaviour (F-05, F-06).** A bad bar, a late Flex report or a server outage on one morning can silently skip the rule for a month.
4. **The G2 gate cannot be evaluated as written (F-09 to F-13).** The trial cap refuses the battery, H12 makes L1 depend on a Stage 2 hypothesis, H11 contradicts H1, and H1's test statistic is not defined.
5. **Contributions and cash are mishandled (F-14 to F-17).** Contributions below about A$1,667 are never invested, the capital cap forces sales of winners, the backtest may credit interest the live account does not earn, and the DHHF benchmark is handicapped.

## Judgment on "a halt blocks risk-off sells"

**Not safe as drafted. Split the halt into two classes.**

- The draft has one `HALTED` state that stops every order. It is set by integrity faults (reconciliation break, breaker trip) and also by performance monitors (drawdown, cost, tracking).
- Blocking a buy leaves the account in cash, which is less risk than the tested rule. Blocking a sell leaves it in the market, which is more risk than anything H1 tested. The two are not symmetric, and the rest of the draft already treats them differently: OPS-04 ("sells proceed regardless"), USR-02, PRT-17, the `TWOFA_MISSED` rule and the sell-veto confirmation.
- `DRAWDOWN_HALT` fires in a deep fall. If the sleeve is still ON, the only practical effect of the halt is to stop the exit the overlay exists to make.
- `COST_HALT` fires after a whipsaw year (about 10 orders at A$5,000, F-04), which is again when another exit is likely.
- For integrity faults, blocking everything is correct: the system does not know what it holds, and a wrong sell is possible. The user must still be told at once and given a manual order sheet.
- Evidence: R20 RND-031 says "halt new orders, hold positions" only against the tracking row. R16's ladder goes flat at −25%. R14 D8 wants a review trigger, not a kill switch. No report says a halt should stop the rule's own exit during a drawdown.

Fix text is in F-02. Open item O3 must be reworded to match.
