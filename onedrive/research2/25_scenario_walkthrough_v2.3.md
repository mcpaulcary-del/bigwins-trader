# 25 — Scenario walkthrough of FD v2.3 (stress test by simulation)

13 November 2026 · reviewer: independent scenario walkthrough · input: `FD_v2.3.md` only, with reports 11–23 used to check facts · the spec was not edited

## 0. Method, assumptions and headline

**Method.** Each scenario was run by hand the way a builder would implement the text: schedule row by schedule row, requirement by requirement, taking the literal reading wherever the spec gives one and recording every place where it gives none. A finding is raised only where the walk produced a wrong result, a result two builders could implement differently, or an unsafe result. Findings already closed by reports 22 and 23 are not repeated.

**Illustrative numbers.** Unit prices are not in any report, so the traces use VAS A$105 and VGS A$145 as round illustrations. Commission is the spec's budget, A$6.60. All dates are checked against the calendar (30 Apr 2027 is a Friday, 31 May 2027 a Monday, 30 Jun 2027 a Wednesday, 31 Dec 2026 a Thursday). Vanguard ex-dates are assumed to be the first trading day of January, April, July and October, which is what report 23 N-09 also assumes; the owner should confirm.

**Headline.** 43 findings: 0 Critical, 7 High, 21 Medium, 15 Low. No walk produced a wrong order that reaches the market unprotected. The seven High findings are places where the normal path halts itself, deploys money before its own checks are done, cannot recover without the builder inventing a rule, or leaves a risk-off sell unsent for days.

**Things the walk confirmed work as written** (no finding): the 01:30 backup and Sunday 03:00 job both exist exactly once on both daylight-saving change days (4 Apr 2027, 3 Oct 2027); the three daily EODHD fetch times fall in one GMT day in both AEDT and AEST and use at most 18 of the 20 calls; the initial-deployment arithmetic of PRT-16(b) gives two orders inside RSK-06, RSK-10, RSK-12 and RSK-13; a manual order-sheet fill is not double-traded by the carried intent; a crash between commit and `placeOrder` gives one broker order; re-entry after a lapsed carried buy or after `resume` is picked up by PRT-16(b); the late-Flex disagreement path (OPS-13 last sentence) cancels unsent orders before 11:30.

## 2. Findings

Severity: **Critical** = a wrong order or wrong money movement reaches the market with no control in the way. **High** = the normal path halts itself, money is deployed before its own checks, a risk-off sell is left unsent, or records of real trades are invented; or the builder must invent a rule to make a listed scenario work. **Medium** = wrong or undefined result with limited money effect, or a routine manual rescue. **Low** = small undefined point.

## 4. Limits of this walkthrough

- Nothing was executed; there is no code. Every trace is a reading of the text.
- Unit prices, Vanguard ex-dates and IBKR behaviour (settled-cash value, Flex timing and date range, statement dates on holidays) are assumptions or open items in the spec (I3, I4, I14). S-02, S-12 and S-13 depend on them; the replacement text is written to be safe whichever way they turn out.
- The tax replacement text in S-17, S-18 and S-19 states an engine rule so that two builders agree; each is marked for the tax agent and is not a statement of the law.
- S-05 and S-20 change operating design values. They need the owner's decision, not only a text edit.
- Stage 2 and Stage 3 paths (N = 3 tranches, P1, the US venue) were not walked.
