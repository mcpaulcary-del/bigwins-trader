# 49. Independent full read of FD v2.11

Report 49, Saturday 14 November 2026. General information, not personal financial advice.

## 0. Result

**FD v2.11 is not ready to freeze as issued. The read found 0 Critical and 2 High findings, 13 Medium and 7 Low (22 in all).** Twenty are corrected in `FD_v2.12.md`; two Low findings are left as they are. Because High findings were found, v2.12 carries the status **Final candidate**, not Final, and no `FD_FINAL_v2.12.md` was written.

The two High findings:

- **W-01, the user's stop.** A `/kill` sent while a sell was planned did nothing until it was sent a second time. The buys of the same plan could go in the meantime, and a `/kill` read at a restart could be lost.
- **W-02, real money.** The system cannot place a real order. It can, though, send the owner a message that names a sell of A200 or BGBL, at about the size of his real holding of the same funds, with nothing that says the message is about paper. That is the only path from this system to a real order, and nothing closed it.

Neither is a wrong order prescribed outright by the text, which is why neither is Critical. W-01 is High on the rubric as written (a builder following RSK-34 could lose a stop). W-02 is High on the rubric's intent: the order would be placed by the owner, not by the builder's code. If the rubric is read strictly as "orders the system itself sends", W-02 is Medium; W-01 keeps the status at Final candidate either way.

## 4. What was checked and found correct

- **No real order.** EXE-12 stops the run unless the connected account id starts `DU` in paper mode; SYS-33 refuses `ibkr.mode: live`; EXE-13 runs advisory mode with the network closed to IBKR. With W-03 and W-04 fixed there is no configuration of the group A or B build that reaches a live account.
- **Halt classes and the kill outside W-01.** `KILLED` and `HALTED_INTEGRITY` block every order; `HALTED_BUYS` and `/pause` let the rule's exits run; the three flags are independent; a cleared halt does not return on the same reading; a resume never releases an order the day it is typed; the out-of-touch kill, the restore rule and the drill-copy rule from reports 26 to 30 are intact.
- **Sells of units not held.** RSK-03 reads the broker position less unmanaged units just before sending; PRT-28 caps a withdrawal sell at the units held above the floor; the send-time quantity uses the managed position everywhere.
- **Bad data.** With choice (a), no order leaves on a single-source close without a valid quote inside 2%, and none leaves on `NO_QUOTE`.
- **Section 17.1 against report 44 section 4.1.** All seven conditions and the earliest date match.
- **Section 20.2 against report 44 section 3.3 and section 5.** Dates and gate conditions match.
- **The Live-Core tags.** 309 requirements, each tagged once; the table in section 20.1 agrees with the rows. No tag was changed by v2.12.
