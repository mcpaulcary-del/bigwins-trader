## Rejected, downgraded or corrected

- **Corrected – `Inactive` status.** IBKR's doc defines it as an order being created and not yet transmitted. The "rejected" meaning I gave comes from third-party clients only. The requirement stands: treat it as needs-review.
- **Corrected – weekly 2FA timing.** Do not hard-code "Sunday 01:00 ET". Sources disagree on Saturday night, Sunday or Monday. Check authentication before every run instead.
- **Rejected – "futures need a margin account at IBKR".** IBKR pages conflict; one implies cash accounts can hold futures with a positive cash balance. Ask IBKR Australia.
- **Upgraded – MGC is deliverable.** CME confirms physical settlement. Keep it off the futures allowlist.
- **Downgraded – US cash-account sizing.** IBKR's own articles conflict on whether unsettled proceeds can be traded at all. Size from settled cash and test on paper.
- **Downgraded to [1]:** Knight "154 stocks", the Aug 2026 IBKR outage, three-strike pacing disconnect, the no-funding secondary user, no-margin-call liquidation, the duplicate-fill anecdote.
- **Still [unverified]:** `ApiPending` meaning; `orderRef`/`permId` persistence across restarts in ib_async; Flex-based ledger rebuild; Risk Navigator limits; micro contract price limits; any IBKR Australia specifics (ASX settled-cash treatment, minimum order sizes, FX). The 2027 daylight-saving dates are my own calculation from the statutory rules, not sourced.

## Scope change: one owner, up to five users, A$5k on ASX ETFs, monthly

- BP-SEC-05 changes from "per-user isolation between two peers" to "per-account configuration under one operator". Whether five Gateway sessions fit on one small VM is untested.
- At A$5k and monthly rebalancing, the dominant risks are operational (authentication, duplicates, bad data, drift), not market-access or margin risk.
- BP-RISK-03 (margin cushion), BP-FUT-01/02, BP-CRY-01 and BP-TIME-01's US daylight-saving cases are deferred until those markets go live.

## BP requirements that matter for the ASX-only live phase, in priority order

1. **BP-REC-01** – reconciliation barrier before any order (rows 9, 11, 13).
2. **BP-ORD-01 and BP-ORD-03** – persisted intent with unique key; never resend on timeout. Add a paper test proving `orderRef` lookup works after a Gateway restart, since row 12 is unverified.
3. **BP-ORD-04** – orders as target minus broker-actual. This matters most for a monthly rule, where a rerun must be harmless.
4. **BP-SESS-01** – authentication check hours before the monthly run, with a page. Reworded to drop the fixed Sunday assumption (row 16).
5. **BP-RISK-01 and BP-RISK-02** – independent breakers and latching halt. At A$5k, set per-order notional and order count tightly; this is the Knight control (row 1).
6. **BP-DATA-01 and BP-DATA-02** – session-complete and bad-bar checks, split detector (rows 3, 4, 30, 31). A monthly rule makes one bad bar expensive.
7. **BP-CASH-01** – size buys from settled cash. ASX is T+2, so a same-day sell-then-buy rotation may be blocked or restricted; test on paper first (rows 20, 22).
8. **BP-OPS-01** – one digest per run plus an external dead-man alert. A monthly system fails silently for weeks otherwise.
9. **BP-SEC-01 to BP-SEC-03** – no-funding bot user, no public ports with egress allowlist, hash-pinned dependencies (rows 7, 27).
10. **BP-OPS-02** – off-site backup and one restore drill before go-live.
11. **BP-OPS-03** – paper, shadow and canary gate. With a monthly cadence, count simulated month-ends, not calendar sessions.
12. **BP-REC-02, BP-ORD-02, BP-SEC-04, BP-TEST-01, BP-OPS-04** – required, but can follow the first live month if the items above are in place.
